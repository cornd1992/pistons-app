# League trade rules (pasted by user 2026-10-17)

Trade rules mirror real-life NBA rules.

## Salary Matching (teams below BOTH aprons after the trade)
| Outgoing salary | Maximum incoming salary |
|---|---|
| $0 - $7,250,000 (note 1) | 200% of outgoing + $250,000 |
| $7,250,001 - $29,000,000 (note 1) | outgoing + $7,500,000 |
| $29,000,001+ | 125% of outgoing + $250,000 |

Using this table hard-caps the team at the First Apron for the rest of the season. If incoming <= 100% of outgoing (table not needed), no hard cap is triggered.

Teams above EITHER apron (status determined AFTER the trade, note 2): max incoming = 100% of outgoing.

## Outgoing vs incoming salary
- Minimum-salary players do not count toward incoming salary for matching.
- Sign-and-trade: outgoing = greater of previous salary or 50% of new salary; incoming for new team = full new salary. (e.g. prev $5M, new $12M -> out $6M, in $12M)
- Poison pill (rookie-scale player with recent extension): incoming = average of current remaining salary + all extension years; outgoing = current salary. (e.g. $5M final rookie year + 4yr/$40M ext -> out $5M, in $9M)

## Who can't be traded
- Recently re-signed: sign-and-trade rules apply. Recently extended: extended-player rules.
- Claimed off waivers: not tradable for 20 in-game days.
- Newly signed: 40 in-game days if signed before training camp; 60 days if signed before camp with Bird/Early Bird and >20% raise; 60 days if signed after training camp.
- Acquired via trade: may be re-traded immediately, but for 40 in-game days cannot be aggregated (must be traded alone), unless acquired into cap room.

## Other rules
- All trades final.
- Max 5 players out per team per trade (cash, picks, stashed players don't count).
- Must have roster space when posting; releases go in Waiver Wire threads and are final.
- Hard-capped team cannot exceed its hard cap amount.
- Can't reacquire a player you traded away (or released) earlier in the same league year.
- Each team must send at least one of: a player under contract, a future draft pick, rights to a stashed overseas player, right to swap unencumbered picks, or >= $110,000 cash (note 3).

## Multi-team trades
- Must list team-by-team what each sends and receives and from whom.
- Salary matching applies to each team individually.
- Each team must send >= 1 asset, receive >= 1 asset, and transact with at least two other teams.
- Each team must send at least one of: player under contract, future draft pick (protections cannot allow conveying nothing), rights to stashed overseas player, or >= $1,100,000 cash.

## Cash
- Minimum $110,000. Maximum = Taxpayer MLE rounded down to nearest million (2005: T-MLE $1,820,000 -> max $1,000,000).
- Can't trade cash you don't have. Teams above Second Apron cannot send cash. Only one team may send cash in a trade.

## Notes
1. Limits increase with the salary cap increase from the previous season.
2. Apron status for trade purposes is determined after the trade completes.
3. First-round picks can never be sold; second-round picks can't be sold during the current Rookie Draft once it reaches round 2.

## Sim schedule
- Trades must be completed before scheduled sim time; later ones process with the next sim. Delayed sim extends deadline unless stated. Commissioner may delay processing trades needing extra moves.
